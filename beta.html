Tutorial

How to activate the beta

It takes about two minutes. You need the beta code we sent you. It looks like BETA-XXXXXX.

  1. Install the beta

    Download it with the button above and open RecipeStudio-Beta-Setup.exe. If Windows says it "protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Follow the setup wizard, and RecipeStudio Beta opens at the end.

    Windows shows that message for new apps from small developers without a paid code-signing certificate. It doesn't mean something is wrong. More about it.

  2. Click Team

    It's in the row of buttons at the top of the app.

  3. Create your account

    Type your email, a password (8 characters or more) and a display name, then click Create account. Already have one? Click I already have an account and sign in.

    The beta is linked to your account, so you can use it on another computer too.

  4. Open your plan

    Click the plan button next to File. It says NORMAL for now.

  5. Enter your beta code

    Under Free test period, type your code and click Join the test.

  6. You're in

    It now says You're a beta tester. Premium and Teams are unlocked until the test ends. Use the Feedback button at the top of the app to tell us what works and what doesn't.

Code not working? Check it's typed exactly as we sent it, and that you're connected to the internet. Still stuck? Email forgetfulstudios@gmail.com.

Good to know

Questions

How do I get a beta code?

Ask us at forgetfulstudios@gmail.com. Codes are limited, so tell us a little about your mod or server.

Can I use it without a code?

Yes. Without a code it works like the free Normal plan.

What happens when the test ends?

The beta stops working on the last day of the test and offers to save your project. Then install the regular version from the download page and open your project there. Your projects are files on your computer, so nothing is lost.

Can I have the beta and the regular version together?

Yes. They're separate apps with their own settings. They share your project files in Documents\RecipeStudio.

What does the beta send to you?

Your feedback, error reports when something breaks, and counts of which features are used. Never your project content. See the privacy policy.
