Refund policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

We want you to be happy with RecipeStudio. The Normal plan is free, so you can try the crafting diagram, the recipe checker and importing before you pay for anything.

Premium (one-time)

If Premium isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of buying it and you'll get your money back in full. You don't need to give a reason. The license key stops working once the refund is made.

Team (monthly)

  • You can cancel any time from Manage billing in the app's Team window, or from the link in your Polar receipt. The team keeps its features until the end of the month you've paid for, and isn't charged again.
  • If you're charged for your first month and change your mind, ask within 14 days for a full refund of that payment.
  • Later monthly payments aren't refunded, except when the law requires it or when you were charged by mistake (for example, after cancelling).
  • Adding a member mid-month is charged for the rest of that month. Removing one lowers the next bill.

When something goes wrong

If a feature you paid for doesn't work and we can't fix it, contact us and we'll refund you, even after 14 days.

How to ask

Email forgetfulstudios@gmail.com with the email address you used to buy and your order number (it's in your receipt). Refunds are made by Polar, our reseller, to the card or account you paid with. They usually show up within 5 to 10 business days.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't take away any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
