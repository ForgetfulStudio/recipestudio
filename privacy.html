Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

RecipeStudio is a desktop app for planning Minecraft mods and servers, made by ForgetfulStudio ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the app and this website use, why, and what you can do about it. In short: your own projects stay on your computer, we only store what's needed for accounts, teams and purchases plus anonymous usage counts you can switch off, and we never sell your information or show ads.

Projects on your computer

Projects you make without a team are saved only on your computer. We can't see them. If you export a file or a web page, it's saved where you choose and only goes where you send it.

Accounts and team projects

To use teams you make an account with your email address, a password and a display name. Team projects, their comments, task assignments, notifications and change history are stored on our server so your team can work together. Your teammates can see your display name and what you add to the team's projects. Change history keeps up to 50 versions of each thing for up to 90 days; notifications are kept for up to 90 days.

If a team owner or editor turns on a share link, anyone who has that link can view that project (read-only) until the link is turned off.

Our server is run by Supabase, which stores this data for us. Passwords are stored by Supabase in a form that can't be read back.

Purchases and licenses

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the seller and handles payment details and tax; we never see your card details. When you activate a Premium license key, the app sends the key and a name for the activation, which includes your computer's name, to Lemon Squeezy to check it. It checks again from time to time. For team plans we store the team's subscription status and number of members.

Anonymous usage statistics

From version 2.6.0, the app sends anonymous usage statistics so we can see which features people use and what to improve. The app tells you about this once, the first time it starts.

  • What's sent: daily counts of which features are used (for example "opened Quests" or "exported a datapack") and how many minutes the app was active. Also the app edition and version, your Windows version, your plan (free, Premium, Team or beta) and whether you're signed in.
  • What's not sent: your name, your email address or anything from your projects.
  • How it's linked: to a random install ID that the app makes the first time it starts. It isn't linked to your account.
  • Where it's kept: on our server at Supabase. Only the RecipeStudio owner can read it.
  • Switching it off: in the app, open File and untick Share anonymous usage statistics. Nothing more is sent after that.

Test versions

If you join a test (beta) version, we store that you're a tester. The test version can also send:

  • Feedback you choose to send, including a screenshot of the app if you leave that ticked.
  • Error reports when something breaks: the error message, technical details and the app version.
  • Usage counts: how often features are used (for example "added a block: 12"), never your project content. You can turn this off in the feedback window.

The regular version of the app doesn't send feedback screenshots or error reports. It only sends the anonymous usage statistics described above.

Updates, downloads and fonts

The app checks our download site for new versions, and the app and this website load fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services see your IP address when your computer connects to them. Our downloads and website are hosted on GitHub.

This website doesn't use cookies, ads or tracking. Only the app sends the anonymous usage statistics described above.

Your choices

  • You can use RecipeStudio without an account.
  • You can delete your comments and leave teams at any time from the app.
  • To get a copy of your data, or to have your account and everything linked to it deleted, contact us at the address below. We'll do it within 30 days.

Children

If you're under 13, or under the minimum age for online accounts where you live, ask a parent or guardian before making an account or buying anything.

Changes

If we change how we use information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
